Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-24

Reze is a personal, non-commercial tool. Its only user is its owner.

Data accessed

With the owner's authorization, Reze accesses the owner's Google Drive, Google Docs, and Google Sheets to create, read, and update the owner's own files.

Use of data

Data is used only to perform the file operations the owner requests. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Storage

Authorization tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer. No data is stored on other servers operated for Reze.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account "Third-party apps and services" page.
